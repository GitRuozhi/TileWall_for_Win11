Website icon provenance

catalog.json records each bundled website icon's content hash, source URL or pinned repository revision, explicit hosts, source dimensions, and source license records. The bundled SOURCE-LICENSE files retain the providers' license texts, and SOURCE-NOTICE files retain their upstream source notices. Item-specific license records and source or guideline URLs are included in the matching catalog entries.

Official-site and official app-listing entries record their original URL and publisher/listing facts. The images identify the linked websites and their respective brands.

Source originals and pinned license documents are retained in the private repository under assets/website-icons. The app uses the packaged local PNG catalog for these bundled icon bytes.
