TileWall Privacy Notice

To display and manage tiles, TileWall reads relevant app information, shortcuts, images, file paths, and the Windows account picture stored on your device. It saves settings, wall data, caches, and logs locally. Import-source caches may include the current Windows account identifier (SID) and local paths. This data is not automatically uploaded to the developer.

Some blur effects temporarily capture an area of the screen, which may include content from other windows. These images are used only for display in memory; they are not saved or uploaded.

Opening web pages or reading network locations may result in network access. Bundled Microsoft components may process usage and diagnostic information; see the [WinUI data-collection notice](https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/blob/main/privacy.md).

Exported wall files may contain images, paths, and URLs and have no additional encryption. Logs may contain paths and error information. Review their contents before sharing.

App data is stored in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\TileWall` and retained after uninstalling. You can delete this folder after fully exiting the app. Separately exported or shared copies must be managed individually.
